1Welcome

PotentialBox turns your Arturia BeatStep Pro into a complete groovebox. The BeatStep Pro is the sequencer and the controller; PotentialBox makes the sound on your Windows PC.

  • Sequencer 1 plays Synth 1, an analog-style synth for basslines, acid, leads and supersaws.
  • Sequencer 2 plays Synth 2, a wavetable synth for leads, pads and bells.
  • The Drum sequencer plays a 16-pad drum sampler: 9 built-in kits, your own samples, and a browser for whole drum machine collections.
  • Each synth has 2 LFOs (with a 16-step mode), a mod envelope, 12 mod slots, seven filter types, chords and an arpeggiator.
  • The Beats tab makes beats in 24 genres and can record them straight into the BeatStep's drum sequencer; the Arrangement tab chains beats into a song.
  • The Live tab has hold-to-play effects, 8 scenes you can morph between, recording to WAV and resampling.
  • The Loop tab is a 5-track looper in the style of a BOSS RC-505.
  • Step locks, mute and solo, side-chain ducking, EQ, delay, reverb and a master effects chain.
  • The BeatStep's 16 knobs control the 16 knobs of whichever page is open, and its pads run actions on MIDI channel 16.

Everything is pre-wired for the BeatStep Pro's factory settings, so you can plug it in and press play. Every control is a big key you can tap, and everything saves by itself: there is no typing anywhere in the app.

New in 1.9.2: cleaner sound (no more crackles from the master effects), a real look-ahead limiter, a playable Arrangement, one-tap mute and solo, 8 working scenes, LOAD and DROPOUTS on the Setup tab. The full list is in the CHANGELOG file that comes with PotentialBox.

3Installing

  1. Download PotentialBox-1.9.2-windows.zip from the Releases page of the PotentialBox GitHub repository.
  2. Right-click the zip and choose Extract All. Open the extracted PotentialBox folder.
  3. Double-click Install PotentialBox.bat.
  4. Wait for the window to say DONE. PotentialBox opens by itself, and there is a PotentialBox icon on your Desktop and in the Start menu (with a shortcut to this manual).
“Windows protected your PC”? PotentialBox is free and not code-signed, so Windows may show this the first time. Click More info, then Run anyway.

What the installer does

  • Installs a private copy of Python 3.12 in your user folder if you don't have it. It doesn't change any other Python you have.
  • Copies PotentialBox to %LOCALAPPDATA%\PotentialBox and installs its libraries there: numpy, scipy, sounddevice (with ASIO), mido and python-rtmidi.
  • Lists the ASIO outputs and MIDI inputs it can see, so you know the BeatStep was found.
  • Adds the Desktop and Start menu shortcuts, then starts the app.

Updating

Download the new release and run its Install PotentialBox.bat. The installer closes PotentialBox if it's open, updates it and keeps all your settings, sounds, kits, setups and loops.

Uninstalling

Open %LOCALAPPDATA%\PotentialBox, right-click uninstall.ps1 and choose Run with PowerShell. This removes the app, its shortcuts and your saved settings, sounds, kits and setups, so copy them first if you want to keep them (chapter 23). Your recordings and loops in the Music folder stay. Python 3.12 stays too; remove it in Windows Settings > Apps if you like.

4First start: Setup

Open the SETUP tab (bottom right) the first time you start PotentialBox.

Audio output

Tap an output to use it. The list is sorted best first:

Output typeWhen to use it
ASIOLowest lag. Choose your audio interface's own ASIO driver. PotentialBox picks it by itself the first time, ahead of generic drivers like ASIO4ALL.
WASAPI EXCLUSIVEVery low lag with no extra driver. Other apps go quiet while PotentialBox uses the output.
WASAPIWorks everywhere, with a little more lag. Other apps keep playing.

Buffer, lag, load and dropouts

  • BUFFER sets the lag: smaller is faster. 128 or 256 is a good start; 1024 and 2048 are there for slow PCs.
  • LAG shows the result in milliseconds.
  • LOAD shows how much of each buffer's time the sound takes to make. Keep it well under 100% (it turns red above 70%).
  • DROPOUTS counts the buffers the sound card had to play without fresh sound. Each one is a crackle. If it climbs, tap the next buffer size up.
ASIO busy? Many interfaces let only one app use their ASIO driver at a time. If a DAW, FL Studio or Voicemeeter is open, PotentialBox can't get the driver, so it plays through Windows audio and tells you why. Close the other app, then tap the ASIO output again.

If an output ever freezes, PotentialBox remembers it, skips it next time and marks it (DID NOT RESPOND). Tapping it again tries it once more.

MIDI in

PotentialBox finds the BeatStep Pro by itself and listens to every port it offers. Tap a port to switch it off or on, and tap RESCAN MIDI after plugging in. The channel boxes set which MIDI channel plays each instrument (factory: Synth 1 = 1, Synth 2 = 2, Drums = 10).

Look

The LOOK key (bottom right) steps through the looks: Print Plate (the default), Night Rack, Colour Block and Vintage (used for the pictures in this manual). The change shows at once and is remembered. If you make a file called user-look.json in %APPDATA%\PotentialBox, it becomes a fifth look the next time PotentialBox starts; any colour it leaves out comes from Print Plate.

5Setting up the BeatStep Pro

The sequencers

Nothing to set up: on factory settings Sequencer 1 sends on channel 1, Sequencer 2 on channel 2 and the Drum sequencer on channel 10 with notes 36-51, which is exactly what PotentialBox listens to.

  • Press PLAY on the BeatStep Pro. PotentialBox follows its tempo (shown in the header), so the delay, LFOs, arp and step locks always land on the beat.
  • Stop also sends All Notes Off, so no note hangs.
  • Tied and slid steps glide from note to note. Set the slide time with the synth's Glide knob.
  • Swing, randomness and probability are the BeatStep's own: they change the pattern before PotentialBox plays it.

The knobs: Control Mode

The knobs only send to PotentialBox in Control Mode. Press CONTROL MODE on the BeatStep Pro; the sequencers keep playing. Turn a knob and the matching knob on screen lights up.

If a knob does nothing, open SETUP, tap LEARN KNOBS and turn knobs 1 to 16 in order (top row first). FACTORY CCs undoes this. If your knobs send relative values (set in MIDI Control Center), switch KNOB MODE to RELATIVE.

The pads: channel 16

To use the pads for pad actions (chapter 22), set the BeatStep Pro's Control Mode pads to MIDI channel 16 in Arturia's free MIDI Control Center app. Out of the box pads 1-16 send notes 36-51, which PotentialBox already expects. Any other notes or CCs work too: assign them in SETUP > PAD ACTIONS.

6The screen

Header

  • AUDIO: the output in use and its lag. Messages also pop up here for a moment.
  • MIDI: whether the BeatStep Pro is connected. The light flashes when MIDI arrives.
  • TEMPO: the tempo PotentialBox is playing at. LEVEL: the output meter (red means you are pushing the limiter or soft clip).

Tabs and pages

The tabs along the bottom choose what the 16 knobs control. The knob area is laid out like the BeatStep's knobs: top row 1-8, bottom row 9-16, in labelled groups. Some tabs have more than one page: the page keys sit at the top of the knob area, and tapping the tab again goes to the next page.

Using the controls

  • Tap the left or right half of a knob to step it down or up, drag up and down, or use the mouse wheel.
  • The coloured ring round a knob shows its value. Settings that go both ways (pan, EQ, tuning) fill from the top.
  • Settings that are just on or off (mute, solo, hard sync, arp hold, glide mode) are one-tap keys instead of knobs. The BeatStep knob for that spot still switches them.
  • A knob that a macro is moving shows a small ring where your own setting is.
  • STEP LOCKS (top right of the knob area) gives a knob a different value on each step: chapter 19. A small L on a knob means it has step locks.

An analog-style synth: two oscillators that blend from saw to square, a supersaw, a sub oscillator, a resonant filter with its own envelope, and drive. It is played by Sequencer 1.

The Synth 1 tab has eight pages: MAIN, OSC+, MOD, MOD2, ARP+, ENV and the two LFO step pages. This chapter covers MAIN, OSC+ and ENV; chapter 9 covers modulation and chapter 10 the arpeggiator.

9Modulation, step LFOs and chords

Modulation makes a sound move by itself: a wobbling filter, vibrato, a sweeping wavetable, auto-pan. Each synth has its own, on its MOD, MOD2 and LFO step pages.

The movers

  • LFO 1 and LFO 2: Sine, Triangle, Saw up, Saw down, Square, Random, Smooth random or Step. Set the speed in Hz, or lock them to the tempo with sync (4 bars down to 1/32, plus triplets).
  • The mod envelope rises and falls once on every new note: sweeps, zaps and plucks.
  • Four more sources need no setting up: the amp envelope, the step's velocity, the note itself (higher notes give more) and Random (a new value on every note).

Mod slots

Each synth has 12 mod slots; each connects one source to one target. Slots 1-8 are the bottom row of the MOD page and slots 9-12 start the MOD2 page. Each knob is labelled with its routing, for example “LFO 1 > Cutoff”.

  • All amounts start at 0, so nothing moves until you turn one up. A negative amount turns the movement upside down.
  • Tap a slot's label to choose a different source and target. Slots 9-12 start empty.
  • INIT MODULATION puts both LFOs, the mod envelope and all 12 slots back to how they started.

Step LFOs

Set an LFO's shape to Step and it plays the 16 knobs of its step page (LFO1 STEPS or LFO2 STEPS) one after another, from -100% to +100%: a little 16-step sequence for the filter, the pitch, the pan or anything else.

  1. On the MOD page, set LFO 1 shape to Step.
  2. Route LFO 1 somewhere in a mod slot (slot 2 is LFO 1 > Cutoff out of the box) and turn its amount up.
  3. Open the LFO1 STEPS page and set the 16 steps.
  • While the BeatStep plays, step 1 is the first 16th after you press Play, and the steps follow its clock.
  • When it's stopped, the steps run at the LFO's own speed or sync (one cycle = all 16 steps).
  • Each step glides into the next over 3 ms, so even big jumps don't click.

Chords

The BeatStep's sequencers play one note at a time, but Chord on the OSC+ page turns every step into a chord: Octave, Fifth, Fifth + octave, Major, Minor, Sus2, Sus4, Major 7, Minor 7, Dominant 7, Minor 9, Stack. Chord voicing spreads it: Close, Open, Wide. All the chord's notes go through the same filter and envelopes, like a classic paraphonic synth.

Heavy patches (chords and supersaw on both synths with lots of modulation) use more of your PC. Watch LOAD on the Setup tab and choose the next buffer size up if you hear crackles.

Using the arpeggiator

  • Turn Arp on and play a long note on the BeatStep (a tied step or a long gate). The arp plays from it.
  • With Chord on (OSC+ page), the arp plays the chord's notes one at a time: the classic way to get arpeggios from a one-note sequencer.
  • Short BeatStep steps also work: each new step plays the next note of the pattern, so a plain 16-step sequence walks through the chord.
  • INIT ARP turns the arpeggiator off and resets it in one tap. Synth 2's ARP+ page works the same way.

Drum knobs

The Drums 1-8 and Drums 9-16 tabs each have four pages:

  • MAIN: a tune knob (semitones) and a length knob (shortens the sound) for each pad.
  • MIX: a level and a pan knob for each pad.
  • GROOVE: chance and plays-when for each pad (below).
  • CHOKE: a choke group (1-8) for each of the 16 pads. When a pad plays, it cuts off every other pad in its group: put an open hat and a pedal hat together, or a long 808 and a second 808.

Chance and conditions

These make a short BeatStep pattern feel longer and more alive, like Elektron's trig conditions.

  • Chance: at 100% the pad always plays. At 70% about 3 hits in 10 are skipped, at random. Great on hats and percussion.
  • Plays when: Always, Fill only, Not in fill, 1st of 2, 2nd of 2, 1st of 4, 2nd of 4, 3rd of 4, 4th of 4, 1st of 8, 8th of 8.
  • 1st of 4 means “only in the first bar of every four”: build a crash on bar 1 or a fill on bar 4 from a one-bar pattern. Bars are counted from when you press Play on the BeatStep.
  • Fill only pads play only while you hold FILL; Not in fill pads drop out while you hold it.

15The Beats tab

The Beats tab makes drum beats in the style of a genre. Tap a genre and you get a beat; tap NEW BEAT for another take, as often as you like. When you have one you like, SEND TO BEATSTEP records it into the BeatStep's drum sequencer, where it carries on like a beat you programmed yourself.

Make a beat

  1. Tap a genre. With GENRE KIT: ON its usual kit loads too, and if the BeatStep is stopped the tempo is set for it.
  2. Tap PLAY. While the BeatStep plays, the beat locks to its clock; when it's stopped, the beat runs on its own tempo (change it with − and +).
  3. Tap NEW BEAT for a different take and MUTATE to change just one part.
  4. BUSIER adds a layer each tap (16th hats, ghost snares, an extra kick, shaker or congas); SIMPLER takes one away. You see each change in the grid.
  5. Tap any dot to remove it, or an empty spot to add a hit.

Length and endings

  • Beats are 2 bars long out of the box. LENGTH switches between 1, 2 and 4 bars.
  • The last bar ends with a change that leads back to the start: a snare roll or pickup, a tom run, an open hat, a kick run, or a drop-out with a crash back in on the one. Its steps are shaded in the grid.
  • NEW ENDING keeps the beat and picks a different ending.
  • In the grid, big dots are accents, normal dots hits, small dots ghost notes, and dots with a ? play about half the time, so the beat never repeats exactly.
  • HOLD FOR FILL plays a fill in the genre's style for as long as you hold it. The mute keys silence the kick, snares, hats or percussion.

Send it to the BeatStep

  1. On the BeatStep, choose the drum sequencer and an empty drum pattern. Set its length to the number of steps shown on screen (32 for 2 bars, 64 for 4) and, if the genre swings, the same swing (shown on the bottom line).
  2. In PotentialBox, tap SEND TO BEATSTEP (or Beats pad 15). It shows WAITING FOR REC + PLAY.
  3. On the BeatStep, press REC and PLAY. PotentialBox sends the beat once, from step 1, on the drum channel.
  4. When it says SENT, press REC on the BeatStep to stop recording. From now on the BeatStep plays the pattern.

While sending, PotentialBox plays the beat itself and ignores the notes the BeatStep plays back, so you don't hear it twice. Maybe-hits (?) are decided once as they're sent, so the BeatStep gets one exact take.

Clock out: PotentialBox sets the tempo

Turn on CLOCK OUT and PotentialBox becomes the clock: it sends MIDI clock, Start and Stop, so the BeatStep plays at the beat's tempo.

  1. Once, on the BeatStep: press its SYNC button until USB is chosen.
  2. Tap CLOCK OUT on the Beats tab (it stays on next time).
  3. PLAY now starts the BeatStep and the beat together; STOP stops both.
  4. Sending is easier too: tap SEND TO BEATSTEP, press REC on the BeatStep during the 4-second countdown, and PotentialBox starts everything and plays the beat in.

To go back to the BeatStep being the clock, turn CLOCK OUT off and set the BeatStep's SYNC back to INT.

Save it as a MIDI file

MIDI FILE saves the beat as a .mid file in Music > PotentialBox Recordings, on the drum channel, with pad 1 on note 36 up to pad 16 on note 51 (the BeatStep's layout), ready for a DAW or an MPC.

18The Loop tab

A 5-track looper, inspired by the BOSS RC-505: record a phrase, it plays round and round, add more on top or on other tracks, and build a song live. It records the inputs of your audio interface (mic, guitar, keys) and PotentialBox's own synths and drums.

First loop in four taps

  1. Tap INPUT OFF at the top right and pick the input your mic or instrument is plugged into (for example IN 1, or IN 1+2 STEREO). This needs your interface's ASIO driver chosen in SETUP.
  2. Press pad 1 (or tap REC on track 1) and play.
  3. Press pad 1 again: the loop plays. Again starts an overdub; once more goes back to playing.
  4. Pad 2 records track 2, and so on. Hold a track's stop pad (9-13) for a second to clear it.

Each track

  • The big key shows what a tap does next: REC, PLAY or DUB. Red is recording, green playing, yellow overdubbing.
  • FROM chooses what the track records: Input, PotentialBox or Both. “PotentialBox” records the synths and drums (with their effects) but not the loops, so nothing doubles up.
  • FX picks the track's effect (Low-pass, High-pass, Lo-fi, Echo, Tremolo); its amount is the track's FX knob.
  • REVERSE plays the track backwards. SPEED switches between 1/2, 1x and 2x. Overdubbing works at 1x.

Timing

  • SYNC TO BEATSTEP: while the BeatStep plays, loop lengths snap to whole bars and the loops follow its Play and Stop, restarting in time with bar 1. With the BeatStep stopped, the first loop sets the length for the rest.
  • START: WHEN I TAP starts recording the moment you press (the end still snaps); START: ON THE BAR waits for the next bar. A press a little late still counts as on the bar.
  • LENGTH: Free, 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars. With a length set, recording stops by itself and the loop starts playing.
  • Your interface's delay is measured and made up for, so what you record lands in time.

19Step locks

A step lock gives a knob a different value on one step of the pattern: a filter that opens on step 4, a pitch drop on the last step, a snare with a longer tail on step 13. The same idea as Elektron's parameter locks.

  1. Open the page with the knob you want, then tap STEP LOCKS at the top right.
  2. The panels at the bottom turn into 16 step keys. Tap a step: it lights up.
  3. Turn knobs (on screen or on the BeatStep). Now they only set that step, and the knob shows LOCK.
  4. Tap another step to lock that one, or the same step again to go back to normal knobs. DONE closes the panel.
  • Locks play while the BeatStep is playing and follow its clock: step 1 is the first step after you press Play, and the 16 steps repeat (for longer patterns the locks repeat every 16).
  • Every knob except the macros, scenes and master volume can be locked.
  • A knob with locks shows a small L. Steps with locks show how many on their key.
  • CLEAR STEP, CLEAR PAGE and CLEAR ALL remove locks. The INIT keys and RESET ALL TO INIT clear the locks they cover.
  • Locks are saved with your settings and in My Setups, but not in My Sounds.

24Troubleshooting

The header says AUDIO ERROR

Open SETUP and tap another output. If your interface's ASIO driver won't open, close any DAW, FL Studio or Voicemeeter that might be using it, then tap it again. WASAPI always works as a fallback.

I hear crackles

Look at LOAD and DROPOUTS on the Setup tab. If DROPOUTS climbs, tap the next BUFFER size up (256 or 512). If LOAD is high, ease off chords and supersaw on both synths, or close heavy apps.

MIDI says NO BEATSTEP FOUND

Check the USB cable, wait a few seconds after plugging in, then tap RESCAN MIDI in SETUP. A DAW can hold the BeatStep's MIDI port open; close it and rescan.

The sequencers play but the knobs do nothing

Press CONTROL MODE on the BeatStep Pro. Still nothing? SETUP > LEARN KNOBS.

The pads don't run actions

Set the BeatStep's Control Mode pads to MIDI channel 16 in MIDI Control Center, or teach PotentialBox your pads in SETUP > PAD ACTIONS.

Step locks, step LFOs, the arp or “1st of 4” don't follow the BeatStep

They follow the BeatStep's MIDI clock. Press Play on the BeatStep and check the header shows its tempo. Stop and Play again to reset the step count.

With CLOCK OUT on, the BeatStep doesn't start

Its SYNC must be on USB. In SETUP, make sure the BeatStep's MIDI port is on.

SEND TO BEATSTEP says no MIDI out was found

Check the BeatStep is plugged in and switched on, then try again. The beat goes out on the drum channel set in SETUP (10 out of the box).

The scene morph does nothing

Save two different scenes and check FROM and TO point at them. The line under the morph bar says how many settings move.

The Loop tab says INPUT OFF, or lists no inputs

Inputs come from the output chosen in SETUP. Pick your interface's ASIO driver there (WASAPI outputs have no inputs), then choose the input on the Loop tab.

A synth or drum sounds wrong

A macro may be turned up (tap ZERO ALL MACROS), a track may be muted or another soloed (Mix > MUTE/SOLO), or the master effects may be on (FX > MASTER). For a fresh start, RESET ALL TO INIT.

A machine kit has odd sounds on some pads

The sorter goes by file names. Use EDIT PAD to swap any pad, then save it in My Kits.

PotentialBox won't start

Run the installer again. If it still won't start, the last lines of %APPDATA%\PotentialBox\log.txt say why; include them if you report a problem on GitHub.
